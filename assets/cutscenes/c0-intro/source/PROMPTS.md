# C0 intro generated sources

Built-in image generation was used for the project-bound source images. The final runtime files are
deterministically cropped, nearest-neighbour resized, and quantized by
`scripts/prepare-c0-intro-assets.py`.

## client-memory-triptych-generated.png

Three separate framed charcoal commissions on a late-19th-century office wall: a missing terrier,
a young adult daughter who moved away, and a deceased elderly husband. Refined 16-bit pixel art,
muted brown/teal/cream palette, warm lamp from below, rainy-night ambience, no text or UI.

## wrong-face-consequence-generated.png

A police portrait of one man on aged paper beside a visibly different innocent man behind holding-cell
bars. Serious late-19th-century pixel-art evidence composition, cold cell light, no readable text,
weapons, gore, UI, or watermark.

## office-closed-door-generated.png

Precise-object edit of the approved rainy office background. Only the right doorway is closed with a
period Victorian dark-walnut door: four recessed panels, carved molding, aged brass knob and key plate,
frame contact shadow and a cool wet threshold reflection. The camera, furniture, portraits, palette and
pixel density remain consistent; no characters, text or UI.
